# Prospective SCF intervention timing panel

Freeze the decision engine at qe-scf-slim v0.4.0 (846f93ab4711752df2c66519844b1add1e68a4b8). Do not change rules or omit a case after seeing an outcome.

Seven physical settings: diamond Si (known healthy), fcc Al, bcc Na, ferromagnetic bcc Fe, rocksalt MgO, six-layer Al(111) with two-vector mixing (known difficult), three-layer Na(110) with two-vector mixing (held-out stress). The other bulk inputs use an eight-vector history. Cases are selected scientific examples, not samples drawn at random. Default refers to the beta; other parameters are explicit.

Run three cold-start repetitions of two settings per case: A uses beta 0.70 and B uses 0.20. Both use 40 electronic steps, 1e-8 Ry, fixed geometry, identical other inputs and no inherited save data. Reverse beta order in round 2, rotate case order between rounds. For any case that fails A, also measure fresh E (beta 0.70, 80 steps) in each round. If A succeeds, its observed time also represents the stop-at-convergence 80-step policy, assuming a higher inactive cap changes nothing. Use one MPI rank and one thread for QE and math libraries, serial solver calls, monotonic complete process elapsed time (launcher/IO included, excludes queue and human time). Per-launch wall timeout 360 seconds, including startup; timeouts/failed runs count. Record all executions. Numeric acceptance requires a completed finite SCF and exit 0; for comparable converged arms report final energy discrepancies, and magnetic moment if relevant.

Replay fixed one-fallback policies on the same observations. (1) A alone. (2) A followed by B on nonconvergence. (3) A followed by E on nonconvergence. (4) A plus offline triage: repeated_rebounds -> B; improving_at_limit/short_iteration_budget -> E; generic/review -> manual review (unresolved, no invented saved outcome). No new QE work for 'none'. Report both completion counts and total spent on all attempts, and count the CLI triage overhead separately and in policy 4. Controls have free knowledge of completion; triage pays for running the CLI. Signed differences are paired, not independent timings. Also report B alone and a direct longer-budget counterfactual as context. No savings claim from an unfinished job. No state transfer to restarts. No automatic killing or timing credit for partial log hints.

Two injected incidents are reported separately: Si with electron_maxstep=2 and Fe with a bad UPF filename (hold all else fixed). Resolve the budget by restoring 40 and the UPF by restoring the known correct file. Compare immediate targeted repair vs one blind beta retry then repair; distinguish actual costs from paired reuse of the corresponding normal A. These known faults are not estimates of how frequent such incidents are.

Primary endpoint: completed tasks and end-to-end solver+diagnostic wall time over one instance of each case per round; report three totals, medians/ranges, matched counts, line-number evidence and explicit manual reviews. Time the frozen executable for terminal batch triage, also time parsing each log alone for latency. Include original stdout and input, checksums, parameters and a script for independent replay. Human time/queue time/surfaces energies in production remain unmeasured.
